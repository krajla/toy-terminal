1. SUMMARY
   A grey quadruped, approx. 5 kg, with folded ears and amber eyes.
   Local residents call it "Luna". It answers to nothing. It doesn't have to.

2. OBSERVATIONS
   2.1  Feeds at 0900, 1500 and 2100. If the food is late, it screams.
   2.2  Most nights at 0300 it runs from the hallway to the bedroom and back,
        six to eight times. On 11/14 it knocked over a ████.
   2.3  Sits facing the northeast corner of the living room, up to 40 minutes.
   2.4  Pushes objects off the desk while it looks at the observer: pens,
        a cup, a phone.

3. HISTORY
   The entity appears in household records from before the household existed.
   Photographs show the same cat in ████ and in ██████████.
   Recorded deaths: 0. Expected: 9.
   Time is a flat circle. Everything it has done, it will do again:
   the feeding, the corner, the edge of the table.

4. ASSESSMENT
   Do not approach during feeding hours. Do not approach outside feeding hours.
   Containment is not possible. The entity sleeps on the containment unit.
